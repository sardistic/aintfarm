how it works

Each particle is its own neural net. Nets start random (untrained) and are never trained by gradients. Selection is what shapes them.

Particles never interact directly. They read, absorb from, and write to a shared G×G×F field. Channel 0 is energy from drifting suns. Channels 1–7 are information. A hidden density channel records how crowded each cell is.

Chemistry is a per-channel rule: an absorb rate, the energy gained or lost per unit absorbed, and a push along that channel's gradient.

Effect genes are 4 slots. Each holds one of 16 primitives: taxis, swirl, isoline-seeking, catalysis (a→b), jet, anchor, pressure/cohesion, pulsed emission, siphon at range, signal relay (a→b), heat, toxin shield, global drift, gene uptake, gene injection, and kin bonding. Every slot has its own channels and parameters, and its strength is gated by the net on each step. Mutation can rewrite a slot into any effect, rewire its channels, or retune it.

Gene transfer and bodies. Uptake copies an effect gene, and sometimes a block of weights, from whoever shared your cell last step. Inject writes your gene into them, like a plasmid or virus. Bond springs you toward neighbours whose inherited bond key nearly matches yours and matches their velocity, so kin can hold together as multicellular bodies.

Learning within a lifetime. Each particle has an evolved learning rate. When it is non-zero, the net adjusts its own hidden-to-output weights every other step with a reward-modulated Hebbian rule: actions that raised its energy above its recent average get strengthened. Children inherit the learned weights (Lamarckian), so evolution and learning stack.

The net sees the field and its gradients at an evolved range, plus density, energy, velocity, 2 memory registers it writes itself, and an internal clock. It outputs split, child share, write, aim, memory, and 4 effect gates. The hidden width mutates.

The gene bank is a vector database of evolved nets. Every second, the GPU samples genomes that have reproduced. Each is embedded as a 49-dim vector of chemistry, effects, architecture, and a random projection of its weights. A new genome that lands within the niche radius of an existing entry replaces it only if it's fitter (more offspring). Otherwise it becomes a new niche. The map is a live PCA of that space. Selected species can be planted, and immigration re-injects archived species to stop monocultures. The bank persists in this browser.
